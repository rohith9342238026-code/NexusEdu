Security & Role Architecture

Strict Separation of Student & Admin Authority

Built with enterprise security in mind. Students and administrators operate on entirely isolated authorization layers. Administrative capabilities are never accessible via ordinary student accounts.

Zero Password Exposure

Admin panels allow student management and verification, but plain passwords remain strictly concealed and protected.

Robust Route Guarding

Protected views inspect active session claims and redirect unauthorized traffic immediately to proper login screens.

Audited Administrator Actions

Student account creations, status modifications, and records updates are validated for non-duplicate keys and clean schemas.
